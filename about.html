ABOUT ROHAX.CC

What we're building.

rohax.cc is a simple, searchable index of free assets, plugins, tools, and executors for Roblox and other games — no accounts, no paywalls, just a clean list of what's out there and a link to get it.

Just an index

We don't host files ourselves. Every entry links out to its original source so you always know where a download actually comes from.

Verified badges

Entries marked "Verified" have been manually checked by the team. Anything unverified is shown as-is — always use your own judgement.

Community-driven

New entries and updates come from our Discord community. Join in to suggest something, report a dead link, or flag an issue.

How it works

Browse by game in the sidebar, or use the search bar to find something by name. The separate "Executors" section under Tools lists standalone tools that aren't tied to one specific game.

Everything here is free. If a listing ever asks you to pay, log in with your credentials, or download something outside the linked page, treat it as suspicious and let us know on Discord.

Disclaimer

rohax.cc is an index, not the author or host of the linked content. We're not affiliated with Roblox Corporation, Mojang, or any other game studio referenced here. Use third-party tools at your own risk and always check a game's terms of service before installing anything.
